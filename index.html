About The Makerspace

Located on the Rye Country Day School campus, the Makerspace is a collaborative hub where Middle and Upper School students design and build projects across disciplines.

Add PNG: images/decor-3.png

From high-precision laser cutting and 3D printing to textiles and physical prototyping, the space is fully equipped with advanced fabrication technology alongside essential electronics and crafting tools. Whether coding microcontrollers or working with traditional materials, students turn innovative ideas into physical projects.
